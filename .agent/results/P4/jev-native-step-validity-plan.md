## Minimal Direction

The existing Noul asks whether a candidate implements *the* next
requested operation, although its result is consumed as primitive
validity. Validity is not a contest to be the uniquely preferred next
step. A preparation step can be implied by an unfinished multi-step
goal without being literally named.

Clarify only that existing predicate, retaining unfinished-work,
exact-control, no-repeat, literal, spatial-evidence and permission
boundaries. Keep the separate Choice and every existing numeric gate.
Do not add Noul heads or a planner.

Also canonicalize the two existing goal-fact JSON-string encodings
with the platform JSONEncoder's sortedKeys option. This repairs
reproducibility, not an established product reliability or speed gain.
Preserve every string's raw UTF-8 and every array's order.
